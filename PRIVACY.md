# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

DWT – Das Wetter tickt does not collect analytics, create user accounts, or share
personal data with the developer.

Location access is optional and is used only to center the radar map on the
watch. The most recent location may be stored locally on the device so the map
can open quickly. Location data is not sent to the developer or included in
requests for radar data.

The app connects directly to the Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) WarnWetter
service to download radar and cloud data. These network requests are subject to
DWD's privacy practices and may expose standard connection information such as
the device's IP address to DWD.

Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data.
