# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

DWT – Das Wetter tickt does not collect analytics, create user accounts, or share
personal data with the developer.

Location access is optional and is used only to center the radar map on the
watch. The most recent location may be stored locally on the device so the map
can open quickly. Location data is not sent to the developer or included in
requests for radar data.

The app connects directly to the Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) WarnWetter
service to download rain, cloud, and lightning data. These network requests are
subject to DWD's privacy practices and may expose standard connection
information such as the device's IP address to DWD.

An optional Android companion can download and process rain frames on the
paired phone. The watch requests a weather asset and timestamp and receives a
processed image and its static geographic bounds through the Wear OS Data Layer.
User location, map camera, and viewport are not included in these requests.
Cloud and lightning processing remains on the watch. Without a compatible,
reachable companion, the watch uses its direct DWD connection.

Data Layer transport is managed by Google Play services and can use Bluetooth
or an encrypted cloud connection. The phone's DWD requests are also subject to
DWD's privacy practices and may expose standard connection information.

Optional local performance diagnostics contain weather-frame timestamps, timing,
cache sizes, and transfer sizes. They contain no user location, account,
credentials, or weather payloads, and are not uploaded by the app.

Uninstalling either app removes its locally stored data on that device.
