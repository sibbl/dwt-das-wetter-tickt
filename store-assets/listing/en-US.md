# Google Play Listing (English)

## App name

DWT – Das Wetter tickt

## Short description

Rain, clouds, and lightning on your Wear OS watch.

## Full description

IMPORTANT: DWT is an independent app. It does not represent the German Weather
Service (DWD) or any other government entity, and it is not affiliated with or
endorsed by DWD.

Official source of the government weather information shown in this app:
https://www.dwd.de/DE/leistungen/opendata/opendata.html

Check current rain, cloud, and lightning data directly from your wrist.

DWT – Das Wetter tickt is a standalone Wear OS app with an animated timeline of recent
observations and forecasts. Use the crown or touch controls to move through
time, pan the map, change zoom levels, and switch between rain, cloud, and
lightning layers.

Optional location access centers the map on your position. The app also works
without location permission.

Data basis: German Weather Service (DWD), WarnWetter raster data reproduced
visually.
